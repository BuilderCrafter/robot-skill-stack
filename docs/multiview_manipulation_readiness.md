# Multi-view manipulation readiness

Primitive detection, pose accuracy, identity continuity, grasp feasibility and actual held-state verification are separate outcomes. Direct manipulation and recovery remain available; general autonomous multi-view manipulation is not established. Do not bypass intended-contact/carrying restrictions in global motion.

Current configuration and retained checks are documented in
[perception](perception.md), [multi-view](multiview_rgbd.md),
[motion planning](motion_planning.md) and [validation](validation.md).

The detailed historical study and its experiment commands remain on the preserved
[source checkpoint](https://github.com/BuilderCrafter/robot-skill-stack/blob/cdd2be9bfa6e95295c83b41d69819579b6dcde94/docs/multiview_manipulation_readiness.md).
Those research drivers are excluded from main's supported test commands.
